## Corrections and retention

Use this path when a correction, source conflict, environment fact or conclusion
could change current work or prevent future rediscovery. Handle it even without
a request to remember it. Include its content and
its source in the `material` input described in [ordinary use](ordinary.md#current-material-and-needs).
Apply trusted corrections to the task. Follow the returned request for the source
that needs changing, or select a relevant procedure from `activation`. One-off
information may need no saved record. Ask only when intended future scope remains
unclear; do not turn a task-local fact into policy.

For correction destinations and bounded owner changes, use the [correction procedure](../../workspace-instruction-correction/SKILL.md). It also supports current material owner friction and repo-directed opportunities including positive simplification or automation opportunities without a failure. Awareness does not grant authority: current latitude selects repair/adapt, report/opportunity or no-action. Keep the requested task primary. Weak cosmetic findings need no record, retrospective or retention; absent material pressure, no reflection phase is required. Resolved pressure becomes quiet through the changed receiving source, not an event ledger.

Use the owner's exact returned request/action and verify its outcome before claiming the correction was retained. If the native owner or supported persistence path is unavailable, surface the exact owner/path gap and state that retention is not established. Do not substitute an apology, chat promise, Memory note, invented persistence, or a direct managed-state edit for correction reconciliation. Repository visibility alone does not establish retention, including when executable AW is unavailable.

## Avoid unnecessary records

- Do not read broad module state just because a capability exists.
- Do not create Planning, Memory, proof, handoff, or local scratch artifacts unless they have a real owner and future decision value.
- Keep arbitrary temporary task material in bounded `.agentic-workspace/local/scratch/` containers; keep structured owner roots clean. Use the shared [task resource procedure](../../workspace-resources/SKILL.md) for scratch or necessary isolation, including terminal cleanup and interrupted recovery.
- Prefer the smallest sufficient query, skill, source read, or bounded operation.
- Stronger future agents may use less procedure when they already know what is relevant; durable policy/currentness/authority boundaries still apply.
