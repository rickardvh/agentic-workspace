# Execution plans

Planning owns bounded execution records and their explicit issue, lane and continuation relations. Use an execplan when milestones, handoff or restart cost justify durable custody; direct work does not need one.

Follow the workspace-startup skill. When current Planning state matters, resolve `agentic-workspace start --target . --task "<task>" --format json` and inspect relevant exact owner references. Current selection is worktree-local. Legacy aggregate queue files and historical summaries are not current selection authority.

Create, select, update or transition managed records only through current Planning requests and exact admitted actions. Templates describe record structure; they do not grant write authority. Read the current schema and requested fields rather than copying an old plan's shape or a retired command example.

A useful bounded plan identifies:

- the original intended outcome, parent intent and negative requirements;
- the current slice, its scope, dependencies and immediate next action;
- milestones, blockers, evidence expectations and escalation conditions;
- required continuation, its owner and activation trigger;
- delivered results, validation, unresolved obligations and durable residue.

Prefer one active milestone and a clear next action. Preserve original scope when tightening means or proof. Prep-only work does not authorize implementation. Assignment policy governs executor choice; a plan or skill does not grant delegation authority.

After a slice, reconcile progress and continuation through the current owner. Validation, issue completion and parent intent satisfaction are separate judgments. Source admissions and independent review remain separate obligations. Do not infer parent completion from a passing local check or an empty owner selection.

Keep durable technical knowledge in its canonical source or Memory. Retain only Planning evidence that has execution, audit or recovery value. Inspect exact archive references only when relevant; do not scan history during ordinary startup or keep a second aggregate backlog.

Native unavailability leaves current admission unknown. Preserve managed records and use only repository-readable facts until the current owner is available.
