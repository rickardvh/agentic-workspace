# System-intent reference

The canonical ordinary procedure is `../skills/workspace-startup/SKILL.md`.
This is a domain reference, not another startup or operating manual.

Repository-owned intent sources are declared in `.agentic-workspace/config.toml`
under `[system_intent]`. Read their current bytes for meaning. The native
System-intent owner supplies current source observations and exact requests
through `start`; follow those requests when reconciliation is needed.

A recorded interpretation or source revision is an assertion with a semantic
lifetime. Do not overwrite interpreted mirrors, advance an admission revision,
or infer successful reconciliation from a query. Unsupported reconciliation
remains an explicit owner gap. Preserve useful prior direction and unresolved
questions under their current owners until deliberately dispositioned.

Task intent remains with Planning. Promote only future-useful durable direction
through its responsible current owner; generic file preservation is not a reason
to keep obsolete procedural machinery active.
