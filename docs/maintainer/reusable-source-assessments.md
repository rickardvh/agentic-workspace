# Reusing source assessments

Verification's source-reconciliation groups describe repository sources. Their
semantic basis includes the governing declaration and relation, exact governing
and read dependencies, covered consumer postimages, producer and applicable
decision authority. Task and Planning identity remain in invocation and claim
evaluation; they are not dependencies of this repository assessment.

An unchanged group is reused without another request, confirmation, publication
or receipt. Reuse supplies no task-completion, command-proof or independent-review
authority. A later task still needs its own claim judgement where required.

Each group's exact Configuration delegation is reobserved against that group's
scope. Adding a consumer does not revoke an unchanged group's matching grant.
New membership gets its own assessment, with the existing delegated decision or
bounded domain judgement. The acting agent checks the supplied sources and exact
resulting work, then confirms or defers its assessment through the returned
request. Recording that assessment requires no human rubber stamp and attests no
human authorisation or independent review. Revoking a grant invalidates dependent coverage while
preserving groups that do not use it.

`coverage.groups` identifies current and non-current receipts and their reasons.
`assessment_basis` names the repository assessment, relation, producer and task
identity's separate role. Detailed retained bindings remain in the exact receipt.
Missing or corrupt current evidence stays a gap; historical files do not recreate
the current projection. Interrupted publication still uses its existing custody.

## Bounded reproduction of #3677

The existing grouped-currentness native test publishes five groups covering 271
consumers, changes task text between fresh processes, then selects a Planning
subject through the native owner. All five groups remain current. It checks zero
source-reconciliation requests, decisions or actions and identical bytes in the
proof subtree across the Planning operation. No new human answer is submitted
for this reuse.

Changing one consumer leaves four groups current. Adding another consumer makes
the prepared old proposal stale; publishing the changed/new group preserves the
other four. A governing-source change invalidates the genuinely dependent scope.
The authority test separately adds a consumer outside an existing exact grant,
checks the bounded domain judgement for that new group, and revokes/adds the grant
to invalidate only the group that depends on it.

Focused controls retain exact publication, protection, interrupted-effect
recovery, missing/corrupt current evidence and governing-source reverse scope.
A native basis test checks producer, semantics, authority and dependency drift.
This is deterministic current-process and fresh-process evidence; it makes no
provider-cost or independent-acceptance claim.

The producer changed in this fix, so incompatible earlier receipts remain stale.
The fix does not migrate old judgements or restore the five receipts retired in
the original incident. New assessments still require their actual authority;
once current under this producer, unrelated task changes no longer invalidate them.

## Domain assessment without a human approval loop

The repository owner explicitly changed the product rule on 27 September 2026:
regular operation resolves through agents and domain owners, with human escalation
limited to Verification steps requiring human judgement and tasks explicitly
configured for human owners. #3692 records that accepted direction. This replaces
#3677's human fallback for new source assessments; it is an authority change in
the decision foundation below #3689's proof-executor repair.

Source reconciliation records the assessor's bounded judgement. Its v5 basis
distinguishes that judgement from human authorisation while retaining exact
proposal confirmation, stale-answer rejection, protected publication and effect
recovery. Historical receipts retain their original meaning for custody checks;
they cannot become current v5 evidence merely by changing the authority label.

The existing 32 source-reconciliation cases passed after the change. A fresh
reader given only the ordinary returned packet identified the agent as the
assessor, required no human answer, and correctly separated publication from
independent review and completion. The reader needed no clarification about the
decision, but could not substantiate the assessment without its source material.
That exercise establishes intelligibility, not correctness of the assessment.
