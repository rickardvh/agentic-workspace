# Compare the requested outcome

Read only relevant current Planning/Verification detail, the user request,
upstream issue and applicable source intent. Extract original intent, interpreted
local intent, parent/larger intent, non-goals, negative invariants, escalation
triggers and continuation owner. Compare the proposed or delivered slice to that
whole outcome. Classify satisfied, partial, unsatisfied, deferred-with-owner or
escalation-required with evidence and an explicit bounded reason.

Validation proves only its observed behavior. Do not claim broader satisfaction
from a narrower proxy. Parent intent left open or stale, missing negative-invariant
disposition, missing current external evidence or missing required continuation
remains unresolved. Preserve source currentness and native claim restrictions.
This judgment can inform shaping as well as completion; no skill owns its admission.
