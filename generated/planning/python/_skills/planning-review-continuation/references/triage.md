# Receive and classify current findings

Read applicable repository instructions, the exact review scope/artifact and only
relevant current Planning detail. Preserve reviewer identity, reviewed source/revision,
scope and provenance. Verification/review owners determine independent eligibility,
evidence and approval; Planning and optional skills cannot authenticate those facts.

Keep the review bounded to its subsystem, question or risk. For an authorized
portfolio, retain compact per-mode artifacts rather than one enlarged dump. Use
the repository's current review template/capture route when a record is required.
Do not hand-edit managed owner state; if its writer is unavailable, report the
proposed finding and exact gap without claiming retained review custody.

Keep only evidence-backed findings, with confidence, source class, concrete target
and promotion trigger. Distinguish friction-confirmed evidence from analysis. A clean
review should say no material findings rather than pad the artifact. Static-analysis
output needs triage. Findings do not activate plans, prove a fix or close claims.

After authorized fixes, reconcile spent findings through the responsible artifact
owner, preserving required evidence and unique rationale. Reporting should identify
scope, artifact, current findings, source classes and any proposed continuation.
