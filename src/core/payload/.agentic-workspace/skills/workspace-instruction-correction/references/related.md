# Use relevant retained findings and reconsider their consequences

Use during ordinary work when facts already selected or read from current sources
could together change an imminent action or claim. Also use after a verified
correction when selected advice may now be obsolete. Keep the original user
outcome primary; do not run the [on-demand review](review.md) or enumerate other
owners merely because some knowledge exists. An unrelated or no-signal task
needs no extra call, record or reflection.

## Make the affected decision explicit

Name the design, edit, check or claim that these facts could change. Compare their
exact sources, scope, dependencies and authority with the controlling repository
source under the [evidence rules](../../workspace-startup/references/evidence.md).
Use sufficient current material already available. Obtain only missing or changed
owner information through its returned read/request or selected `detail_refs`;
do not repeat `start` merely to combine facts in reasoning.

Judge whether the observations support one underlying correction, legitimate
alternatives or distinct outcomes. A recorded assertion is not a violated rule;
byte-current advice can still describe an obsolete workaround. Explain the
relationship and uncertainty using the [review's result conventions](review.md#make-a-small-set-of-supported-decisions),
scaled to this decision. Stale or unavailable material leaves the dependent
conclusion unproven; preserve other authorised actions.

Choose one bounded next step through [destination selection](destination.md) and
[effective latitude](opportunity.md). A direct correction, exact owner action,
existing issue/Planning route, responsible defer or justified no-action can each
be valid. Name its actual source/owner and proof. Finding a relationship grants no
new write authority, cannot silently narrow the original task and cannot turn a
human-owned purpose or equal-authority choice into an inferred requirement.

## Verify the correction, then reconsider only affected material

Check the controlling source's actual postimage and the affected behavior before
claiming a fix. Reassess each selected conclusion that depended on the former
condition. A matched dependency hash, successful edit or refreshed interpretation
alone does not establish substantive resolution or whole-lesson absorption.

Use the receiving owner's current view or continuation to determine what remains.
For affected Memory advice, follow its
[retain/retire/promote request](../../../memory/skills/memory-hygiene/references/disposition.md):
retire an obsolete workaround only with a bounded no-future-value reason supported
by the verified correction; retain useful distinct rationale. Promotion requires
the stronger owner's existing whole-lesson admission. Disposition changes metadata
and preserves the note text; do not delete it or rewrite hashes to suppress it.
Intent interpretations use their own reconciliation request, and Verification
gaps use their own evidence/claim requests. One owner's result settles none of
the others automatically. An unavailable owner leaves its disposition unresolved.

Observe fresh selection for the affected work after an admitted disposition.
Confirm that resolved compensation no longer governs the decision and that
independent safety facts, missing evidence, current commitments and unresolved
choices survive. Changed source, policy or receiver evidence can make advice
relevant again under that owner's currentness rules. Report the exact remaining
gap instead of treating an empty projection as proof of correctness.

Finish the original task and state any materially changed decision, confirmed
source effect and remaining owner consequence. With no useful residual work,
create no issue, plan, Memory note or success record. Reuse existing custody for
unfinished legitimate work; no cross-owner findings store is needed.
