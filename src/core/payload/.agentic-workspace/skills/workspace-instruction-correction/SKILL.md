# Retain repository behavior

Apply trusted corrections to current work. For findings from source/test work, repeated friction, positive opportunities or
conclusions with future value, apply the [evidence boundary](../workspace-startup/references/evidence.md)
and choose the strongest responsible owner. No retention and already-current are valid.
Evidence alone does not establish publication authority.

Use [the selection question](procedure.md), or follow the same source directly:

- [Choose and verify the responsible change](references/destination.md).
- [Publish scoped instructions](references/instructions.md).
- [Advisory knowledge, decisions or method repair](references/other.md).
- [Judge a selected repository opportunity](references/opportunity.md).

Use the configured invocation and [exact owner carriage](../workspace-startup/references/owners.md).
Missing optional procedure grants no policy waiver or alternative writer.
